The ClimatFeux project focuses on the intersection of climate, wildfires, forest ecology,
and people ́s interactions through traditional and contemporary ecosystem
management. The first Work Package is to build a collaborative research network for
applying pyrodendrochronology to investigate climate-fire relationships in the
Mediterranean region. This branch of science applies tree-ring dating to reconstruct
long sequences of fire events for comparison with climate patterns, providing a
fundamental understanding of the role of climate as a fire driver. The second Work
Package is aimed at designing and testing practical interventions that can translate
insights from paleo-historical ecosystem knowledge, such as that described in WP1, to
improve future ecosystem sustainability, and reduce social and ecological risks. In
partnership with collaborating researchers, we will test fire management options such
as ecological restoration, fuel treatments, and prescribed burning using experimental,
observational, and model-based research methods. The third Work Package will form
new partnerships focused on fire ecology and management in several African nations
where fire ecology and management are critical issues, applying appropriate
paleoecological and experimental research techniques. The collective work of our team
will result in new research partnerships producing new data sets, scientific
publications, outreach to professionals and the public, and a PhD graduate as well as a
post-doctoral research experience. These contributions will help support science and
provide useful information for adapting to warming climate and changing fire patterns.
